004 : Himalayan Bird Project

← Back to projects

Project Overview

Conducted ecological data analysis as part of the UCSB-Smithsonian Scholars Himalayan Project, focusing on bird population trends across Eastern and Western Himalayan regions.

Approach

  • Merged bird observation and forest metadata using shared grid identifiers
  • Analyzed bird counts, species richness, elevation, annual temperature range, and precipitation
  • Compared regional biodiversity patterns between Eastern and Western Himalayan grids
  • Created visualizations to communicate ecological trends and data findings

Key Findings

  • Observed more individual birds in the East than the West: about 2,848 vs. 2,120 birds
  • Found higher species richness in the East, with about 216 species compared to 163 in the West
  • Identified elevation, temperature range, and precipitation as useful environmental variables for interpreting bird distribution

Tech Stack

  • R
  • dplyr
  • ggplot2
  • Data cleaning
  • Ecological data visualization
