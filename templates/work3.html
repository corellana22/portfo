003 : Global Happiness Project

← Back to projects

Project Overview

Analyzed World Happiness Report data to study how economic, social, and regional factors relate to national happiness scores across countries. The project focused on using statistical modeling and visualization to explain differences in well-being across regions.

Approach

  • Cleaned and explored country-level happiness data
  • Used regression modeling to examine relationships between happiness score and key predictors
  • Included region-specific interaction terms to compare how predictors varied across global regions
  • Created visualizations to communicate trends, distributions, and model results

Key Takeaway

The project showed that predictors of happiness do not affect all regions equally, highlighting the importance of regional context when interpreting global well-being data.

Tech Stack

  • R
  • ggplot2
  • dplyr
  • Regression modeling
  • Data visualization
